## Design Specificity Verdict

Split down the middle. Four things could not be lifted onto another clinic: the hero line "Tired, flat, and told you're fine?"; the interactive anatomy figure with MEN/WOMEN toggle; the verification ledger in DoctorCard.tsx (every claim links the regulator that could refute it; reviews gated behind REVIEWS_LIVE=false); PanelMarkers.tsx printing every marker on both panels by sex.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The funnel states its own length three times and is wrong all three times
Hero.tsx renders "Step 1 of 5". ShortAssessment.tsx computes totalSteps = 7 (from a hero chip) or 8 (direct) and labels "Question 1 of 8". PlaceScreen says "Two more, then we show your match" when FIVE screens remain. ProgressBar reaches 08/08 with three screens to come. Both surfaces promise "two minutes"; measured ~1s blank transition per advance on top of eight questions.
Why: audience is defined as people already dismissed by a GP, skeptics wanting clinical credibility. The first checkable claim the site makes about itself is false, and the next two corroborate it.
Fix: export TOTAL_STEPS from ShortAssessment.tsx, have Hero.tsx read it; count place+privacy into the bar or relabel; compute PlaceScreen's remainder. Command: /impeccable clarify

### [P1] "You're a strong match" is a clinical-suitability verdict produced from nothing
LoadingScreen runs a 2.6s requestAnimationFrame ring cycling "Reviewing your answers / Matching your pathway / Checking eligibility / Preparing your next step" while computing NOTHING, then the eligible phase renders a "PATHWAY MATCHED" badge and "Based on your answers, you're a good candidate for the Hormone Optimisation pathway." No answer influences any of it - Assessment A skipped every question 4-8 and got the identical verdict. One screen earlier PrivacyScreen promises "A doctor decides. An AHPRA-registered doctor reviews you. Not an algorithm."
Why: a suitability representation about a named individual, delivered by theatre impersonating clinical triage, on an AHPRA/TGA-regulated surface. PRODUCT.md principle 3: show the pathway, never imply a result. LARGEST COMPLIANCE EXPOSURE ON THE SITE.
Fix: delete LoadingScreen; replace the verdict headline with a pathway statement; move "a doctor decides, not an algorithm" onto this screen. Command: /impeccable harden

### [P1] .t-eyebrow fails WCAG AA everywhere, against the project's own written rule
--blue resolves (styles/tokens.css:242) to #4890f7. Measured: 3.18:1 on #ffffff; 3.0:1 on band-light #f6f8fa; 2.94:1 for the pill variant (worst on the site); .label at 10px same colour; Logo wordmark at 7.5px nav / 10px footer same colour. Browser detector independently reported 3.2:1, corroborating.
DESIGN.md states the rule verbatim: "never raw #4890f7 for small text", and names the fix (#1d4ed8, 5.9:1, already --color-accent-fg).
In use on seven homepage sections, every /start StepHeading, and Pricing.tsx's highlighted card label.
Fix: .t-eyebrow { color: var(--color-accent-fg) } plus the Pricing label. Command: /impeccable polish

### [P1] Prices are set in a dot-matrix face and are the least readable numbers on the page
Pricing.tsx applies t-readout (Doto, font-variation ROND 100) to $199 / $99 / $99 at clamp(36px,3.6vw,50px). The $ reads as a plus-with-dots; 9s carry a centre dot and scan as 8s. The plain-Inter qualifier beneath ("member - $280 single visit") is materially easier to read than the price it qualifies.
Why: audience defined as comparing Apex against Biov8/Hormn/Everlab in adjacent tabs. Price is the comparison variable, rendered as ornament.
Fix: keep Doto for atmosphere numerals (48h, Day 03, 90d); set actionable numbers (all three Pricing values, "From $199" rows, ProgressBar counter) in Inter with tabular-nums. Command: /impeccable typeset

### [P1] The conversion funnel leaks, and the page's own chrome competes with it
/start renders the full site <Nav />: Treatments dropdown, How it works, Pricing, About, Patient Portal, and a SECOND "Start your assessment" button - six exits from a funnel that persists no state. Refresh or any nav click destroys eight answers, no warning, no resume. Separately the inline <style> hiding chat-widget/#ghl-chat-widget does not match what ships: a "START HEALTH ASSESSMENT" pill floats bottom-right over the primary Continue button, offering what the user is already doing.
Fix: on /start replace <Nav /> with a logo plus a single "Save and exit"; persist answers to sessionStorage keyed by phase and restore on mount; fix the widget selector. Command: /impeccable harden
